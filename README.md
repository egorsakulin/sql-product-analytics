# SQL Product Analytics Case Study

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes transactional and customer data from a Brazilian e-commerce platform using SQL and Python.

The goal of the analysis is to explore customer behavior, revenue trends, product performance, and retention patterns in order to generate actionable business insights.

The project demonstrates practical SQL skills commonly used in product and business analytics roles.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze order and revenue trends
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Calculate key business metrics
- Explore customer retention behavior
- Perform cohort analysis using SQL

---

## 📊 Dataset

The analysis uses the **Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist**, available on Kaggle.

The dataset contains anonymized real-world e-commerce data, including:
- customer information
- orders
- payments
- products
- order items

Source: Kaggle — Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist

URL: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce

## 🔑 Key Insights

- The platform experienced strong growth in both orders and revenue over time
- Home and lifestyle product categories generated the highest number of purchases
- Average Order Value (AOV) reached **160.99 BRL**
- Customer retention dropped sharply after the first purchase
- The business appears to rely heavily on new customer acquisition rather than repeat customers

---

## 💼 Business Interpretation

The analysis suggests that while the platform demonstrates strong monetization and growth potential, customer retention remains a major challenge.

Potential opportunities for improvement include:
- loyalty programs
- personalized recommendations
- re-engagement campaigns
- improved customer experience
